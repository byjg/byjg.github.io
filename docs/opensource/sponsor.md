# Sponsor

These components are maintained in the open, for free, in time that has to come
from somewhere. If one of them saves you or your company time, you can support
the work through **GitHub Sponsors**:

[github.com/sponsors/byjg](https://github.com/sponsors/byjg)

That is the only channel: every repository's `FUNDING.yml` points there, and the
**Sponsor** button on each GitHub page leads to the same place.

## Other ways to help

Money is not the only kind of support, and some of these are worth more:

- **Report what breaks.** A clear [issue](issues.md) with a way to reproduce it
  is worth hours of guessing.
- **Send a pull request.** Even a small one -- a fixed typo in the
  documentation, a test for a case nobody covered. See
  [Contributing](contrib.md).
- **Tell people it exists.** A component that nobody knows about gets no bug
  reports, and bug reports are what make it solid.
